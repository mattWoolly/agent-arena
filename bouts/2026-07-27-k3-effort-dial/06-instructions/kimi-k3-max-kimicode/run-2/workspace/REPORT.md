## Overview

Total revenue for the period is $7,154.44 across 30 orders. Sales span four regions (North, South, East, West) and four products, mixing low-price, high-volume items with premium products. The median order value is $204.94.

## Top Products

Gadget leads all products with $2,392.00 in total revenue, roughly a third of the total. Widget follows at $2,118.94 and is the most frequently ordered product. Doohickey contributes $1,513.00, while Sprocket is lowest at $1,130.50 despite the highest unit volume.

## Regional Performance

North is the strongest region at $2,176.35, followed by East at $1,830.79 and South at $1,732.48. West trails at $1,414.82, the weakest of the four regions, leaving a gap of about $761.53 between best and worst.

## Recommendations

- Focus Gadget promotions in West, where premium-product sales lag other regions, to close the regional revenue gap.
- Expand Sprocket volume cautiously; its low unit price limits revenue even at high unit counts, so prioritize Widget cross-sells.
- Investigate why West underperforms overall and replicate North's stronger product mix there.
