## Overview

Total revenue across the period was $7,154.44 from 30 orders, spanning
2026-05-02 through 2026-05-21. The median order value was $204.95, and four
products sold across four regions.

## Top Products

Gadget led on revenue with $2,392.00 from just 16 units across 7 orders, driven
by its $149.50 unit price. Widget followed at $2,118.94 from 106 units in 10
orders, making it the most frequently ordered item. Doohickey contributed
$1,513.00 from 17 units, and Sprocket $1,130.50 despite moving 238 units — the
highest volume and the lowest revenue, at $4.75 per unit.

## Regional Performance

North was the strongest region at $2,176.35 over 8 orders, helped by two Gadget
orders and heavy Sprocket volume. East followed with $1,830.79 from 7 orders,
then South at $1,732.48 from 8 orders. West trailed at $1,414.82 from 7 orders,
its lowest-priced mix among the four. The gap between North and West is
$761.53, roughly 11% of total revenue.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize Gadget inventory and promotion: 7 orders produced 33% of revenue,
  the highest revenue per order at $341.71.
- Review Sprocket pricing; 238 units, a third of all volume, returned only
  $1,130.50, the weakest revenue of the four products.
- Apply North's product mix to West, where 7 orders yielded $1,414.82 against
  North's $2,176.35.
