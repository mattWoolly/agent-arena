## Overview

Total revenue across the period was $7,154.44 from 30 orders. The median order
value was $204.94, indicating a spread between small unit purchases and a few
high-value transactions.

## Top Products

Gadget led with $2,392.00 in revenue, followed by Widget at $2,118.94.
Doohickey contributed $1,513.00 and Sprocket trailed at $1,130.50. Gadget and
Widget together account for roughly 63% of total revenue, so the catalogue is
concentrated in two lines.

## Regional Performance

North was the strongest region at $2,176.35, ahead of East ($1,830.79) and
South ($1,732.48). West was weakest at $1,414.82 — about 35% below North.
The four regions are otherwise fairly close, with no region contributing less
than 19% of the total.

## Recommendations

- Protect Gadget supply and pricing; it alone drives $2,392.00, the largest
  single share of the $7,154.44 total.
- Investigate West, which produced $1,414.82 versus North's $2,176.35, to find
  whether coverage or demand explains the gap.
- Test bundling Sprocket ($1,130.50) with Widget or Gadget orders to lift the
  $204.94 median order value.
