## Overview

Total revenue across the period was $7,154.44 from 30 orders. The median order
value was $204.94, and four products sold into four regions.

## Top Products

Gadget led with $2,392.00, followed by Widget at $2,118.94. Doohickey
contributed $1,513.00 and Sprocket $1,130.50. The top two products together
account for roughly 63% of total revenue, so performance is concentrated in a
narrow part of the catalog.

## Regional Performance

North was the strongest region at $2,176.35, ahead of East ($1,830.79) and
South ($1,732.48). West trailed at $1,414.82, about 35% below North. The spread
between best and worst region is $761.53, a gap wide enough to matter but not
so wide that any region looks unviable.

## Recommendations

- Protect Gadget and Widget supply; together they drive about 63% of revenue,
  so a stockout in either would hurt disproportionately.
- Investigate West, the lowest region at $1,414.82, to learn whether the gap
  reflects fewer orders or smaller average order size.
- Test whether Sprocket, last at $1,130.50, deserves continued shelf space or
  repricing against the stronger three products.
