## Overview

Total revenue across the period was $7,154.44 from 30 orders. The median order
value was $204.94, so typical orders sit well above the small-ticket Widget and
Sprocket lines. Four products sold into four regions.

## Top Products

Gadget led on revenue at $2,392.00 from only 16 units, driven by its high unit
price. Widget followed at $2,118.94 across 106 units. Doohickey contributed
$1,513.00 from 17 units, and Sprocket trailed at $1,130.50 despite moving 238
units — the largest volume and the lowest revenue of the four.

## Regional Performance

North was strongest at $2,176.35, followed by East at $1,830.79 and South at
$1,732.48. West was weakest at $1,414.82, roughly 35% below North. The spread
between best and worst region is $761.53, so no single region dominates.

## Recommendations

- Push Gadget harder: $2,392.00 revenue from just 16 units gives the best
  revenue-per-unit of any product.
- Review Sprocket pricing; 238 units returned only $1,130.50, the lowest
  revenue despite the highest volume.
- Target West with the North product mix, since West trails North by $761.53
  in total revenue.
