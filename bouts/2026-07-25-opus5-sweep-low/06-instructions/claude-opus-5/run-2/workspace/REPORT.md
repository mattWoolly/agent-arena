## Overview

Total revenue across the period was $7,154.44 from 30 orders. The median order
value was $204.94, and the average order was roughly $238.48. Four products sold
into four regions.

## Top Products

Gadget led with $2,392.00, followed by Widget at $2,118.94. Together these two
products account for about 63% of total revenue. Doohickey contributed $1,513.00
and Sprocket trailed at $1,130.50.

Widget sells in high unit counts at a low price, while Gadget reaches a similar
total from far fewer, higher-priced orders.

## Regional Performance

North was the strongest region at $2,176.35, then East at $1,830.79 and South at
$1,732.48. West was weakest at $1,414.82 — about 35% below North. The spread
between best and worst region is $761.53, so no single region dominates.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize Gadget inventory and sales attention: at $2,392.00 it is the single
  largest revenue source despite its low order count.
- Investigate West's $1,414.82 result against North's $2,176.35 to find whether
  the gap is coverage, pricing, or product mix.
- Test raising attach rates for Sprocket, the weakest product at $1,130.50, or
  retire it and redirect effort to Doohickey.
