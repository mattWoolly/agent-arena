## Overview

Total revenue across the period was $7,154.44 from 30 orders. Average order
value was $238.48 and the median order value was $204.94, indicating a fairly
even spread with a few larger orders pulling the mean above the midpoint.

## Top Products

Gadget led with $2,392.00, followed closely by Widget at $2,118.94. Together
these two products account for roughly 63% of total revenue. Doohickey
contributed $1,513.00, while Sprocket trailed at $1,130.50 — the only product
below the $1,200 mark.

## Regional Performance

North was the strongest region at $2,176.35, ahead of East ($1,830.79) and
South ($1,732.48). West was weakest at $1,414.82, about 35% behind North.
The gap between the top and bottom region is $761.53, so demand is spread
across all four territories rather than concentrated in one.

## Recommendations

- Protect Gadget and Widget supply; at $4,510.94 combined they carry roughly
  63% of revenue and any stockout would hit hardest.
- Investigate West's $1,414.82 result against North's $2,176.35 to find whether
  coverage, pricing, or product mix explains the shortfall.
- Review Sprocket's $1,130.50 contribution; either reposition its pricing or
  shift shelf space toward the two leading products.
